connect_inventory
/datum/component/connect_inventory

Behaves similar to connect_loc_behalf, but hooks into signals on items in the user's inventory

Vars

allowed_slotsBitfield of the slots items have to be in to get the signals registered
connectionsAn assoc list of signal -> procpath to register to the items in the tracked inventory
trackedThe mob whose inventory we are watching

Procs

handle_tracked_qdelDeletes the component when the tracked mob gets deleted
on_equipped_itemRegisters the connections to a newly equipped item if it went into an allowed slot
on_unequipped_itemUnregisters the connections from an item once it is dropped
unregister_signalsUnregisters the connections from every item the tracked mob has equipped
update_signalsRegisters the connections to every item currently in an allowed slot
