The emagged spiderbot - a spiderbot chassis whose access controller has been overwritten with a cryptographic sequencer.

The unit answers to whoever swiped the card, hits harder than a stock chassis, and detonates when it is destroyed. Applied and cleared by /mob/living/basic/spiderbot/proc/apply_emagged_directive and its counterpart.
