regenerative_extract
/datum/status_effect/regenerative_extract

Heals the owner over time, then leaves a cooldown that weakens the next extract.

Vars

base_healing_amtThe damage healed (for each type) per tick. This is multipled against the multiplier derived from cooldowns.
diminish_timeHow long the subsequent cooldown effect will last.
diminishing_multiplierThe multiplier that the cooldown applied after the effect ends will use.
extra_traitsExtra traits given to the owner, added to the base traits.
given_traitsBase traits given to the owner.
multiplierThe number multiplied against the base healing amount, used for the "diminishing returns" cooldown effect.
nutrition_heal_capThe maximum nutrition level this regenerative extract can heal up to.

Procs

apply_alert_effectTints the alert to match the extract's color.
heal_actDoes one tick of healing, using every heal_* proc below.
heal_damageHeals brute, burn, oxygen, toxin, and clone damage.
heal_miscRestores blood and nutrition, and lowers disgust.
heal_organsHeals every organ, and cures brain traumas.
heal_woundsRemoves the most severe wound.
