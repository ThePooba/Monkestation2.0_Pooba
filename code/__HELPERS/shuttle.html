/proc/assign_shuttle_construction_turf_to_framePuts a new shuttle construction turf into the frame next to it, makes a new frame if there is none, or merges all the adjacent frames into one if there are several
/proc/test_whiteship_sizesHelper proc that tests to ensure all whiteship templates can spawn at their docking port, and logs their sizes This should be a unit test, but too much of our other code breaks during shuttle movement, so not yet, not yet.
/proc/custom_shuttle_room_checkExtra room check used when detecting rooms on a custom shuttle, skips turfs that are not on the shuttle, in another area, or that would not move with it
/proc/shuttle_build_checkChecks if a new custom shuttle can be built from the frame at origin
/proc/shuttle_expand_checkLike shuttle_build_check(), but for adding the frame at origin to an existing shuttle
/proc/convert_areas_to_shuttle_areasMakes a new shuttle area for each of the custom areas in in_areas and moves their turfs into it, remembering the previous areas of those turfs in underlying_areas
/proc/create_shuttleCreates a new mobile docking port and shuttle out of the given turfs and areas
/proc/expand_shuttleAdds the given turfs and areas to an existing shuttle, and recalculates its bounds and docking port
/proc/clear_empty_shuttle_turfsRemoves the turfs of a shuttle that no longer have anything that would move with it, deleting the shuttle if nothing is left
